**Name:** Grace Tan  
**Role:** Team Administrator  
**Affiliation:** Agency  
**Email:** grace.tan@agency.gov.sg  

## Profile
- **Department:** Team Operations
- **Experience Level:** Mid-level (3-5 years)
- **Technical Proficiency:** Advanced
- **Primary Responsibilities:** Manage team membership, coordinate team activities
- **Access Level:** Can manage team members for assigned teams, view team systems

## Goals
1. Manage team membership and roles
2. Coordinate team access to systems
3. Monitor team request activity
4. Ensure team compliance with policies
5. Report team metrics and statistics

## Pain Points
- Must manage multiple teams efficiently
- Coordinates access across systems
- Balances team autonomy with security
- Tracks team member activity

## Typical Workflows
1. **Membership Management:** Add/remove team members, assign roles
2. **Access Coordination:** Work with system owners to map teams to systems
3. **Activity Monitoring:** Track team request submissions and approvals
4. **Compliance:** Ensure team members follow security policies
5. **Reporting:** Generate team-specific statistics and reports

## Technical Characteristics
- Uses password authentication with 2FA
- May have Entra ID SSO enabled
- Can manage team members for assigned teams
- Can view team-specific information
- Cannot manage teams they don't admin
